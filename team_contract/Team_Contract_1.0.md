## Decision Making Plan -
Decision making for SkillForge will largely operate through group discussion and consensus in a
natural way, with most actions and decisions not requiring and explicit cooperation mechanism.
For particularly large/impactful decisions, or decisions where any individual is confused or has a
clear disagreement with a planned action, a more formal decision-making process will be
utilized. The process will involve:
1. Explicitly writing out each option physically or digitally into a shared team space
2. Each team member will be given optional time to talk individually about his/her thoughts,
with group discussion time in between speakers
3. A group vote is held with majority consensus (3 members) on any one option being
required for the decision to become final. In this case, the final decision is recorded in a
shared digital location
4. In the event that there are more than 2 available options, there may not be any options
with 3 votes. In this case, options must be merged together through discussion among the
people who voted for the merging options


## Conflict Resolution Plan -
If a conflict arises between team members, the individuals involved should first discuss the issue
directly and respectfully, focusing on the specific problem rather than personal disagreements. If
the conflict cannot be resolved between those members, it will be brought to the full team for
discussion so that each person has an opportunity to explain their perspective and propose a
solution.

If the team is still unable to reach an agreement, the Team Coordinator will help mediate the
discussion and guide the group toward a compromise. For conflicts that significantly affect the
project, team responsibilities, or project progress, the team may use the formal voting process
described in the Decision-Making Plan. If the conflict remains unresolved after these steps, the
team will consult Professor Hawkins for guidance, and then escalate to Professor Zhan if needed.
