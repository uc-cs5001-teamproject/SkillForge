## Professional (Manufacturability)
As a predominately AI research focused topic, the project is limited by members expertise with robotics and neural network research fields.  This limits the scope of pure research-based work, while encouraging more surrounding work like a good product website from users with experience with frontend, or a strong backend and computing infrastructure from backend engineers.  This constraint can be mitigated by seeking out external advice through our project advisor and other professors with relevant experience.

## Security
The end goal of the product is to receive money from customers to develop AI models given proprietary video footage of human tasks, introducing security constraints for payment processing and data isolation from being read and stored.  To address this, payment processing must be secure, and payment information will not be stored/remembered after the user has finished using the product.  In addition, data ingested for training will be fully deleted after use.  Finally, data security can be in conflict with economic constraints as compute services that satisfy data isolation needs may be more expensive.
